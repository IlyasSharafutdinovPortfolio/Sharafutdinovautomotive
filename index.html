Privacy Policy

Who we are

This website is operated by Sharafutdinov Automotive Inc., Brickell, Miami, FL, USA (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at Sharafutdinovauto@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Partnership form: your name, email, phone (optional), country and city (optional), area of interest and any message you write.
  • Email you send us directly: your email address and the content of your message.
  • Language setting: your chosen language is saved in your browser (local storage) so the site remembers it. It is not sent to us.
  • Fonts: the site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads.

We do not use advertising cookies or sell your data.

Why we use it

We use your details only to answer your request and to discuss a possible partnership or investment. If you are in the EU or UK, our legal basis is your consent (the checkbox on the form) and our legitimate interest in replying to inquiries.

How the form sends your data

Depending on how the site is configured, the form either opens your own email app with the message prepared, or sends it to us through a form-processing service. In both cases the message reaches our email inbox, which is hosted by our email provider.

Sharing

We share your data only with service providers that help us run the site and handle email, and when the law requires it. Some of these providers are located in the United States, so your data may be transferred outside your country.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiries for as long as needed to handle your request and any resulting relationship, and then delete them or keep only what the law requires.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to withdraw your consent at any time, to object to or restrict processing, to receive a copy of your data, and to complain to your local data protection authority. California residents have similar rights to know and delete their data. To use any of these rights, email us at the address above.

Children

This site is not intended for people under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page.
